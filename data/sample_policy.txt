Company AI Policy

Employees may use approved AI tools to support research, drafting, summarization, coding assistance, and data analysis.

Employees must not upload confidential customer data, private financial records, passwords, API keys, medical records, or legal documents into public AI tools.

AI-generated outputs must be reviewed by a human before being used in business decisions.

Any AI system used for customer-facing decisions must be tested for accuracy, fairness, privacy, and security.

Developers must store API keys in environment variables and must never commit secrets to GitHub.

All AI applications should include logging, error handling, and documentation.
